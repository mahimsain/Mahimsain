⚽ How To Play

🎯 Objective

Score more goals than the other team.

👟 Control

Use your feet, head and body to control the ball.

🤝 Teamwork

Pass, move and communicate with teammates.

🔥 Important Skills

Dribbling

Move with the ball while keeping it close.

Passing

Pass accurately to your teammates.

Shooting

Strike the ball accurately toward goal.

First Touch

Control the ball when receiving it.

📜 Basic Rules

👥 11 players per team in standard football.
⏱️ A standard match has two 45-minute halves.
🚫 Outfield players cannot deliberately handle the ball.
🟨 Yellow card = caution.
🟥 Red card = player is sent off.
🚩 Offside can be called when a player becomes involved in play from an offside position.

👕 Positions

🧤 Goalkeeper
Protects the goal.
🛡️ Defender
Stops attacks.
🧠 Midfielder
Connects defence and attack.
⚡ Winger
Attacks from the sides.
🎯 Striker
Creates and scores goals.
